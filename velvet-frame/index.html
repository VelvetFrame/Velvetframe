About

Velvet Frame exists to preserve what is real.

The quiet moments.
The unspoken connection.
The beauty found in vulnerability.

I'm a photographer, storyteller, witness. My work is rooted in intimacy, atmosphere, and the belief that every person has a story worth remembering.

Every session is designed to feel less like a photoshoot and more like stepping into a visual narrative — where you are not a subject, but a protagonist in your own mythology.
